# MCA Phase 1 - Form Catalogue

**Document Purpose:** Comprehensive inventory of MCA prescribed e-forms and webforms.

**Last Updated:** 27 August 2026

---

## Evidence and Methodology

**Data Sources:**
- Third-party MCA forms lists (2025-2026)
- Companies Act 2013 and Rules (statutory basis)
- LLP Act 2008 and Rules (statutory basis)
- Audit document form taxonomy
- Historical MCA V2 forms references

**Limitations:**
- No direct access to official current MCA forms catalog
- V2 to V3 transition may have changed form numbers/names
- Some forms may be deprecated, merged, or replaced
- New forms may exist post-2021

**Evidence Classification:**
- **VERIFIED:** Form referenced in multiple recent (2025-2026) sources
- **INFERRED:** Form required by statute, likely still active
- **HISTORICAL:** Form documented in older sources, status unclear
- **UNKNOWN:** Form number/name uncertain, validation required

## 1. COMPANY INCORPORATION FORMS

### SPICe+ (Simplified Proforma for Incorporating Company Electronically Plus)
**Category:** Incorporation  
**Evidence:** VERIFIED (Multiple 2025-2026 sources)  
**Purpose:** Integrated company incorporation with name reservation and linked registrations  
**Legal Basis:** Companies (Incorporation) Rules, 2014  
**Status:** PRIMARY incorporation form in V3  

**Integrated Services:**
- Part A: Name reservation (RUN functionality)
- Part B: Incorporation details
- AGILE-PRO: Automatic generation of PAN, TAN
- AGILE-PRO ESIC: ESIC registration
- AGILE-PRO EPFO: EPFO registration

**Filed By:** Subscribers/professionals  
**Entities:** New companies (all types eligible for SPICe+)  
**Authentication:** DSC of applicant  

**Key Data Sections:**
1. Company name and type
2. Registered office details
3. Authorized and paid-up capital
4. Directors (DIN, addresses, DSC)
5. Subscribers and shareholding
6. Main objects and business activities
7. Declarations and undertakings

**Attachments:**
- MOA (Memorandum of Association)
- AOA (Articles of Association) or adoption of model AOA
- Address proof
- Director identity/address proofs
- Declarations

**Fee:** Based on authorized capital  
**Processing:** STP-eligible  
**Outcome:** CIN, Incorporation Certificate, PAN, TAN  
**Typical Timeline:** 2-7 days  

**Validation Required:** Current SPICe+ structure, Part A vs separate RUN, integrated registration scope

---

### RUN (Reserve Unique Name)
**Category:** Name Reservation  
**Evidence:** VERIFIED  
**Purpose:** Reserve company name separately (if not using SPICe+ Part A)  
**Legal Basis:** Companies (Incorporation) Rules, 2014  
**Status:** Active (but may be integrated into SPICe+)  

**Filed By:** Applicants/professionals  
**Name Validity:** 20 days (reapplication allowed)  
**Fee:** Prescribed  
**Processing:** Automated name search + approval  

**Validation Required:** RUN standalone usage vs SPICe+ Part A integration

---

**Validation Required:** Current INC-7 usage scenarios

## 3. DIRECTOR AND KMP FORMS

### DIR-3 (Application for Allotment of Director Identification Number)
**Category:** DIN Application  
**Evidence:** VERIFIED  
**Purpose:** Apply for new DIN  
**Legal Basis:** Section 153, Rule 9  
**Filed By:** Individual intending to be director  
**Authentication:** DSC or self-attestation  

**Note:** Can be included in SPICe+ for first directors  

---

### DIR-3 KYC (Director KYC)
**Category:** Director KYC  
**Evidence:** VERIFIED (Frequently used service)  
**Purpose:** Annual KYC verification for directors  
**Legal Basis:** Section 154  
**Filed By:** Every director with DIN  
**Due Date:** Annually by specified date (typically March/April)  
**Fee:** NIL if on time; late fee if delayed  
**Consequence:** DIN deactivated if not filed  

**Data:**
- Current address
- Current mobile/email
- Current directorships
- PAN verification

**Validation Required:** Current due date, late fee structure, reactivation process

---

### DIR-6 (Intimation of Change in Particulars of Director)
**Category:** Director Details Update  
**Evidence:** INFERRED  
**Purpose:** Update director personal information (name, address, etc.)  
**Legal Basis:** Section 154, Rule 11  
**Filed By:** Director  
**Timeline:** 30 days from change  

---

### DIR-12 (Particulars of Appointment/Cessation of Director or KMP and Changes)
**Category:** Director/KMP Changes  
**Evidence:** INFERRED  
**Purpose:** Report appointment, resignation, removal, or cessation of director or Key Managerial Personnel  
**Legal Basis:** Sections 160, 167, 168, 170, Rule 13

## 4. ANNUAL COMPLIANCE FORMS

### AOC-4 (Filing of Financial Statements)
**Category:** Annual Financial Statements  
**Evidence:** VERIFIED  
**Purpose:** File annual financial statements and auditor's report  
**Legal Basis:** Section 137, Rule 12  
**Filed By:** Company  
**Timeline:** Within 30 days of AGM (or last date of AGM if not held)  

**Variants:**
- **AOC-4:** Regular companies
- **AOC-4 CFS:** Companies filing consolidated financial statements
- **AOC-4 XBRL:** Companies required to file in XBRL format
- **AOC-4 NBFC:** Non-Banking Financial Companies

**Attachments:**
- Balance sheet
- Profit and loss statement
- Cash flow statement
- Notes to accounts
- Auditor's report
- Board's report

**Fee:** Based on authorized capital  
**Processing:** STP + potential e-Scrutiny  

**Validation Required:** XBRL applicability, NBFC specific requirements

---

### MGT-7 (Annual Return)
**Category:** Annual Return  
**Evidence:** VERIFIED  
**Purpose:** File annual return with prescribed details  
**Legal Basis:** Section 92, Rule 11  
**Filed By:** Company  
**Timeline:** Within 60 days of AGM  

**Key Sections:**
1. Company registration details
2. Registered office and contact
3. Promoters and directors
4. Members and shareholding pattern
5. Share capital and debentures
6. Indebtedness
7. Turnover and net worth
8. Board meetings
9. Related party transactions
10. Penalties and compounding

**Attachments:**
- Certificates (CS/CA/CMA)
- Declarations

**Fee:** Based on authorized capital

## FORM LIFECYCLE NOTES

**Form States (INFERRED):**
1. **Available:** Form published and active
2. **Draft:** User started but not submitted
3. **Validated:** Passed all validations
4. **Signed:** DSC applied
5. **Payment pending:** Fee not paid/reconciled
6. **Submitted:** Submitted to ROC (SRN generated)
7. **Under processing:** STP or human review
8. **Query raised:** Requires correction
9. **Approved:** Accepted and processed
10. **Rejected:** Not accepted

**Form Versioning (ASSUMED):**
- Forms have version numbers/dates
- Rules changes trigger form updates
- Old form versions may be deprecated
- Grace period typically provided for transition

**Validation Required:** Actual form lifecycle states, versioning practice
